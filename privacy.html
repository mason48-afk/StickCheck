Privacy and monetization disclosure · Updated October 11, 2026.

Controller data stays in your browser

StickCheck reads gamepad axes, button values, and a browser-provided controller label to run diagnostics. The diagnostic code does not upload these readings. Live movement trails are kept in memory and discarded when you leave.

Saved baselines and downloaded reports

If you choose “Save as before-repair baseline,” we store one report in localStorage on this browser. It includes the reported device label, browser user-agent, selected connection type and axes, test time, reference setting, and summary measurements. Delete it with the baseline delete button or your browser's site-data controls.

Downloaded JSON reports contain the same information and may include your saved baseline. Review the file before sharing it with anyone. Imports are processed locally. Demo results are explicitly marked and cannot be imported as a real baseline.

Hosting and fonts

The hosting service processes normal web requests and may maintain operational logs. Access to this private version is controlled by the hosting platform. The website uses locally bundled fonts; its diagnostic code includes no analytics or advertising trackers.

External providers

Amazon shopping links open Amazon.com, which has its own privacy policies, service terms, and data handling. Your report is not automatically shared when you follow a link.

Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Amazon shopping links are not yet monetized. No commission is currently earned.

Shopping links lead to third-party listings or search results. We do not automatically send your diagnostic report with a link. Prices, availability, compatibility and return policies are determined by the seller. We do not represent Amazon as endorsing StickCheck.

Advertisement spaces are reserved, but Google AdSense is not active. No Google ad requests or advertising cookies are generated by this version. When approved advertising is enabled, this notice will describe Google's processing and the consent choices available.

About this tool

StickCheck is an independent browser-based diagnostic tool, not a controller manufacturer or repair service. Results describe browser-reported input under the test conditions. They do not certify a controller as fault-free or guarantee that a repair is necessary or successful.
